A simplified implementation of the ice nucleation and growth model in Yang et al. (2024).

How this follows the paper
  • Ice formation: AgI deposition nucleation from Eq. (1), applied once per injection.
  • Growth: vapor deposition from Eq. (5), using spherical crystals and a ventilation factor of 1.
  • Falling: the Heymsfield–Westbrook method cited in section 2.3, applied to spheres.

Our simplifications: fixed air properties, still air, no riming or moisture depletion. Below-cloud descent and instant melting at 0°C are our extensions. The animation illustrates a 1D model; radar detection and real-world rainfall enhancement are outside this demo.
